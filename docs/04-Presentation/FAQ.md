# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Overview

This document provides answers to the most frequently asked questions about **WBOS (WhatsApp Business Operating System)**. It is intended to help judges, mentors, recruiters, developers, potential users, and business stakeholders understand the project's vision, architecture, functionality, and future roadmap.

---

# General Questions

## 1. What is WBOS?

WBOS (WhatsApp Business Operating System) is an AI-powered business management platform that transforms WhatsApp into a centralized business operating system.

It combines:

- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- AI-powered assistance
- Marketing Automation
- Workflow Automation
- Business Analytics
- Customer Communication
- Reporting

into a single platform.

---

## 2. Why was WBOS developed?

Many businesses already use WhatsApp as their primary communication platform, but customer information often remains scattered across chats, spreadsheets, emails, and multiple software systems.

WBOS centralizes these processes to improve productivity, customer experience, and business decision-making.

---
