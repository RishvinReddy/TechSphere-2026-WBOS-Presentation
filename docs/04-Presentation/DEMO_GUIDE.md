# Demo Guide

## Overview

This document serves as the official demonstration guide for **WBOS (WhatsApp Business Operating System)**. It is intended for project demonstrations, technical presentations, product showcases, academic evaluations, and investor pitches.

The purpose of the demonstration is not only to showcase the features of WBOS but also to explain the business problems it solves, the technologies behind the platform, and the value it delivers to organizations.

The complete demonstration takes approximately **15–20 minutes**, with an optional Q&A session.

---

# Demo Objectives

The primary objectives of the demonstration are:

- Introduce WBOS
- Explain the business problem
- Demonstrate key features
- Showcase AI capabilities
- Present business analytics
- Explain system architecture
- Highlight future roadmap

# Step 1 — Introduction

Begin by introducing yourself and the team.

Example:

> Good morning everyone.

> We are Team WBOS, and today we are excited to present our project, **WhatsApp Business Operating System**.

Introduce the team members.

- Rishvin Reddy
- Lakshya Bamboli
- Sparsh Jhunjunwala

---

# Step 2 — Problem Statement

Explain the real-world problem.

Example:

Today, most businesses communicate with customers through WhatsApp.

However,

- customer information is scattered,
- follow-ups are manual,
- marketing is inefficient,
- analytics are limited,
- and teams lack centralized management.

Businesses need a smarter solution.

---

# Step 3 — Solution

Introduce WBOS.

Example:

WBOS transforms WhatsApp into a complete Business Operating System by combining:

- CRM
- AI
- Marketing
- Analytics
- Workflow Automation

within one platform.

- Active Leads
- Revenue
