This document contains the official speaker notes for presenting **WBOS (WhatsApp Business Operating System)**. # Slide 2 — Problem Statement

## Objective

Explain why the project exists.

### Speaker Notes

"Today, businesses communicate extensively through WhatsApp.

However, they still rely on multiple disconnected systems to manage customers, sales, marketing, and business operations.

Some common problems include:

- Customer information scattered across different platforms.
- Manual follow-up processes.
- Lack of centralized communication.
- Limited business insights.
- Poor workflow automation.

These inefficiencies reduce productivity and negatively affect customer experience."

### Speaker Notes

# Presentation Tips

## Before the Presentation

- Test the presentation.
- Verify internet connectivity.
- Open all required browser tabs.
- Prepare demo accounts.
- Check audio and display.

---

## During the Presentation

- Speak clearly and confidently.
- Maintain eye contact with the audience.
- Focus on solving business problems rather than only explaining technical details.
- Demonstrate the software whenever possible.
- Avoid reading directly from the slides.

---

## After the Presentation

- Invite questions.
- Thank the audience.
- Collect feedback.
- Note suggestions for future improvements.
